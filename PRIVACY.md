# Hisably — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Hisably is a personal finance app for Android, developed by Muhammad Abrar.
This policy explains, in plain words, what data the app uses and where it
goes.

## The short version

Your money data belongs to you. Hisably has **no server of its own** — the
developer cannot see your accounts, transactions, or Khata records. There
are **no ads, no trackers, and no analytics**.

## Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter — accounts, transactions, Khata (lent/borrowed)
records, budgets, and savings goals — is stored **only on your device**, in
an encrypted database. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this data
is gone unless you made a backup.

## Google Sign-In (optional)

If you tap **Continue with Google**, the app receives your name, email
address, and profile photo from Google and shows them in your in-app
profile. This information stays on your device. The app never receives your
Google password.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you enable backup, the app saves a copy of your data to a hidden,
app-only folder (`appDataFolder`) inside **your own Google Drive**. Only
this app, signed in with your Google account, can open that folder — it does
not appear in your normal Drive files. The developer has no access to it.
You can delete the backup at any time from your Google Drive settings
(Settings → Manage apps → Hisably) or by signing out in the app.

The only information the app ever sends anywhere is this backup file, to
your own Google Drive, when backup is on. Nothing else leaves your phone.

## Permissions

* **Internet** — used only for Google Sign-In and the Drive backup above.
* **Notifications** — used only to tell you when a backup has completed.

## Children

Hisably is a finance tool for adults and is not directed at children.
